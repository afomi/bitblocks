# Ink and wash (bitblocks)

Drawings in qart's ink-and-wash style, the style of the qart `/labs` hero (`labs-bench`).
The recipe lives in qart: `~/workspace/qart/design/ink-and-wash/README.md`.
Same fixed style block, same Grok CLI command, one subject per prompt.

Bitblocks pages are dark, and black ink vanishes on a dark page.
So serve the drawing on its own white paper (`reorg-fork.webp`), as qart does in dark mode.
The keyed copy (`reorg-fork-keyed.webp`, white made transparent) is for a light surface only.
