# Field notes

Dated, small findings from reading the chain with Bitblocks.
They are research notes for civic.studio, not announcements and not a blog.

Each note makes one claim and keeps to this shape:

- **Finding:** the claim, in a sentence or two.
- **Status:** `observed` (read directly from the chain), `inferred` (a reasonable reading of what was observed), or `open`.
- **Method:** how to repeat it, with txids.
- **What this doesn't show:** the limits of the claim.

A note is a record of what was known on its date.
If it turns out wrong, add a dated correction and leave the original text.
Holder addresses are left out of the published data, because a note is about how things happen and not about who it happened to.
